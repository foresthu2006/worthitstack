Metricool vs Buffer: Best Scheduler for Small Teams (2026)

Last updated October 2026 · 10 min read · Pricing verified October 2026

In short: Pick Buffer if you're a solo creator with 1–3 channels — the free plan or $5/channel (annual) Essentials is the cheapest credible scheduler. Pick Metricool for multiple brands or real analytics: the flat ~$20/month (annual billing) Starter covers 5 brands with ad tracking, competitor monitoring, and reports Buffer can't match at any channel count.

Metricool and Buffer both schedule your social posts, and both have generous free tiers. That's where the resemblance ends. Buffer is the simplicity-first scheduler: clean queue, per-channel pricing that starts at $5/month, and the lowest friction in the industry for a solo creator with three accounts. Metricool is the analytics-first platform: scheduling plus ad tracking, competitor monitoring, and custom reports, priced per brand bundle — $20/month annual for up to 5 brands ($36/month annual for up to 10).

The whole comparison turns on one billing difference: Buffer charges per connected channel, Metricool charges a flat rate per brand bundle. At 2 accounts Buffer wins; at 8, Metricool runs away with it. Here's the math and the full breakdown.

The channel math that decides it

Three realistic scenarios, with the numbers (using annual pricing):

Scenario A: solo creator, Instagram + TikTok + LinkedIn (3 channels). Buffer: free plan covers all three (10 posts each) — $0. Metricool: free plan covers 1 brand at 50 posts/month — $0, but LinkedIn scheduling requires the Starter plan ($20/month on annual billing). Buffer wins at zero dollars for the simplest setups.

Scenario B: small business, 5 profiles. Buffer Essentials: 5 × $5 = $25/month ($300/year). Metricool Starter: $20/month ($240/year) — and it adds ad tracking, competitor monitoring, and full analytics Buffer doesn't offer at any tier. Metricool is both cheaper and deeper.

Scenario C: freelancer managing 3 clients × 4 profiles (12 channels). Buffer Essentials: ~$50/month at the volume-discounted rate (Buffer's own published annual totals put 10 channels at $50/month) — on Team, ~$100/month. Metricool Starter: $20/month (annual billing) for 5 brands, or Advanced at $53/month (annual billing) for 15 brands with team approvals. The per-channel meter is Buffer's ceiling for multi-client work; this is exactly why "Buffer alternatives" is a busy search.

The honest crossover point: somewhere around 4–5 channels, Metricool's flat bundle beats Buffer's meter — and the gap only widens. Below that, Buffer's simplicity and free tier are genuinely hard to beat.

Frequently asked questions

Is Metricool really cheaper than Buffer?

It depends entirely on channel count. At 1–3 channels, Buffer is cheaper (free or $5/channel annual). At 5+ channels, Metricool's $20/month Starter beats Buffer's per-channel meter — 5 channels on Buffer Essentials is $25/month, 10 is $50/month. There is no flat answer here; your channel count is the answer.

What's the catch with Buffer's free plan?

Mostly the 10-posts-per-channel queue cap and 3-channel limit — fine for light posting, tight for daily publishers. Also note a connected Start Page (Buffer's link-in-bio) counts as a billable channel on paid plans, which catches people out when budgeting. Light posters never feel those caps; daily posters hit them within weeks.

Can Metricool schedule to LinkedIn?

Yes — but LinkedIn access requires a paid Metricool plan. The free plan excludes LinkedIn, so you need Starter ($20/month on annual billing) or higher. Buffer includes LinkedIn on all plans, including free. If LinkedIn is central to your workflow, budget for the Metricool plan upgrade or lean toward Buffer's free tier.

Does Buffer have analytics?

Yes — post analytics on free, advanced analytics on paid plans. But it's engagement reporting, not the ad-spend tracking, competitor monitoring, or Looker Studio exports that Metricool offers. If you need to know which posts worked, Buffer suffices; if you need to justify spend or benchmark competitors, that's a different depth for a different job.

Which is better for agencies?

Metricool, at these prices. The Advanced plan ($53/month annual) covers 15 brands (scaling to $159/month for 50) with team approvals and reports — the equivalent on Buffer's per-channel Team plan runs several times that. Note Buffer retired its dedicated Agency plan in late 2025.

Can I migrate scheduled posts from Buffer to Metricool?

There's no one-click migration between schedulers — you'll reconnect your social accounts via OAuth in Metricool and rebuild the queue. Let Buffer's scheduled posts run out naturally while you start fresh in Metricool; there's no historical data worth the export hassle for most users.

Final verdict

Worth it for solo creators: Buffer. The free plan covers 3 channels, the interface is the simplest in the industry, and $5/channel (annual) Essentials is fair for small setups. If your social presence is three accounts and a queue, stop here.

Worth it for everyone else: Metricool. At $20/month (annual billing) for 5 brands with ad tracking, competitor monitoring, and real reports, it's the best value in social scheduling for small teams, freelancers, and agencies. The flat pricing is the whole argument — Buffer's meter can't compete past 4–5 channels.

Skip Metricool if you have 1–3 accounts and just need a queue — Buffer's free tier is simpler and free. Skip Buffer if you're managing clients or need analytics with teeth — the per-channel bill scales into agency money without agency features.
