Quick questions, quick answers

The questions people ask most about this comparison, answered directly. Stats and pricing verified October 2026.

Metricool vs Later — which is cheaper?

Entry prices look similar: both Starters are $25/month ($20/month annual Metricool, $18.75/month annual Later). The real difference is billing logic: Metricool bills flat per brand (5 brands on Starter, unlimited users), while Later bills per social set — 1 set = 8 profiles, one per platform.

Can I schedule two Instagram accounts on Later Starter?

No — Starter includes exactly 1 social set, which holds one Instagram profile. A second Instagram account requires a second set, meaning an upgrade to Growth ($50/month, $37.50/month annual).

Is Later worth it if Instagram isn't my main channel?

No. Later is the Instagram-first visual feed planner — the drag-and-drop preview is best-in-class at $18.75/month annual. If your setup spans TikTok, LinkedIn, ad spend, or client reporting, Metricool's $20/month annual for 5 brands is the better pick.

What is the Later social-set billing trap?

Each social set = 8 profiles (one per platform), and Starter covers just one set — growing teams multiply sets fast. Metricool's flat per-brand billing ($20/month annual for 5 brands, unlimited users) is the safer scaling bet unless Instagram is your whole business.

The quick verdict

Pick Later if Instagram is your business — the visual feed planner with drag-and-drop preview and Linkin.bio is genuinely the best at this price, and one social set covers a single-brand setup at $18.75/month annual.

Pick Metricool if your setup has any complexity: multiple accounts on one platform, ad spend to track, reporting clients to serve, or team members who shouldn't multiply the bill. At $20/month for 5 brands with unlimited users, it's the safer scaling bet.

The one-sentence version: Later is a visual planner that charges for bundles; Metricool is an analytics platform that charges flat. Buy Later for the feed preview, Metricool for everything else.

The social-set math that decides it

Later's social set is one profile each from Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, Pinterest, LinkedIn, YouTube, Threads, and Snapchat. Great for a single brand active on many platforms. But it has exactly one Instagram slot — and the 30-posts-per-profile limit on Starter counts at scheduling time, so heavy Instagram posting eats the allowance before the other seven slots see use.

Scenario A: one brand, one Instagram + one TikTok. Later Starter: $18.75/month annual — fits perfectly, and you get the visual planner. Metricool Starter: $20/month annual — also fits. This is Later's happy path, and it genuinely wins here by a hair.

Scenario B: two Instagram accounts (brand + personal), one TikTok. Later Starter can't do it — you need a second full social set, so you're on Growth: $37.50/month annual, paying for 13 platform slots you'll never use. Metricool Starter: still $20/month (5 brands). Later now costs nearly 2× for the same job, and the waste only grows.

Scenario C: 3-person team, 2 brands × 4 profiles. Later Growth gives you 2 sets but only 2 users — the third teammate needs Scale ($82.50/month annual) or an add-on. Metricool Starter: $20/month with unlimited users. Later's user cap turns a team plan into a team tax.

The honest takeaway: Later's pricing assumes your accounts spread evenly across platforms. The moment you stack multiple profiles on one platform — which is exactly what growing brands do — the set model charges you for the platforms you don't need. Metricool's flat per-brand pricing has no such assumption, so it degrades gracefully.

Later's visual moat

The one thing Later does that Metricool can't touch. The drag-and-drop visual planner — rearranging posts to see exactly how your Instagram grid will look, planning campaign sequences by appearance, not just by date — is why Instagram-first brands pick Later. For a clothing brand, café, photographer, or creator, the order of the visuals is part of the story, and Later is the only sub-$20 tool that plans that story. Add Linkin.bio (a real link-in-bio page that tracks which posts drive clicks) and you have the complete Instagram toolkit in one tab.

Metricool's counter — and where it lands blows. The analytics gap is the real one: deeper reporting, competitor benchmarking, the Looker Studio connector, and ad-spend tracking are the things Instagram-focused brands grow into the moment they run paid social or report to clients. Metricool also has the free plan Later lacks — you can learn scheduling for $0 while you decide whether the visual planner is worth paying for. Be honest about the gap: Metricool won't preview your grid, and its Instagram features are functional, not delightful.

Frequently asked questions

Is Later cheaper than Metricool?

Only for the simplest case: Later Starter is $18.75/month on annual billing (1 social set) versus Metricool Starter at $20/month annual (5 brands). But a second Instagram account forces a second Later social set (Growth, $37.50/month annual), which erases the price advantage instantly. Metricool's per-brand pricing scales far more gradually for multi-account setups.

What is a Later social set?

A social set is Later's bundling unit: one profile from each supported platform — Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, Pinterest, LinkedIn, YouTube, Threads, Snapchat — eight profiles per set. Starter includes 1 set, Growth 2 sets, Scale 6 sets. The catch: one profile per platform, so a second Instagram account needs an entirely new set.

Which is better for Instagram planning?

Later, clearly. Its visual planner with drag-and-drop feed preview — seeing exactly how your grid will look before posting — is the best Instagram-first planning experience in this price range, plus the Linkin.bio landing page. Metricool has a competent visual calendar but no feed-preview planning. If Instagram aesthetics drive your business, Later's planner is worth real money.

Does Later have a free plan?

No permanent free plan as of 2026 — Later discontinued it and now offers a 14-day free trial on paid plans. Metricool still has a real free plan: 1 brand, 50 posts a month (LinkedIn scheduling requires a paid plan). You can trial Later free, but you can't park on a free tier.

Which is better for agencies?

Metricool for most agencies: the Advanced plan ($53/month annual) covers 15 brands with team approvals and white-label-style reports, scaling to 50 brands, with unlimited team members on every tier. Later's sets get expensive fast — 6 Instagram brands needs 6 sets on Scale ($82.50/month annual) — and Starter/Growth include only 1–2 users.

Can Metricool or Later track my ad spend?

Metricool. Its analytics edge explicitly includes ad-spend tracking, alongside competitor benchmarking and a Looker Studio connector — the pick when paid social is part of the workflow. Later's moat is the visual Instagram-first planner with feed preview and Linkin.bio; ad analytics aren't its strength. At $20/month annual for 5 brands, Metricool is the ad-runner pick.

Does Metricool have a link-in-bio feature?

No — Metricool has no link-in-bio equivalent. That's Later's territory: Linkin.bio is bundled with its Instagram-first visual planner at $18.75/month annual. Metricool answers with analytics depth instead — competitor benchmarking, ad-spend tracking, the Looker Studio connector. Pick Later if the grid and bio link are your business; Metricool for analytics.

Can I use Metricool's free plan forever?

Yes — Metricool still has a real free plan: 1 brand, 50 posts a month. Later discontinued its free tier and now offers a 14-day trial only, so free-only schedulers have no reason to shortlist it. Note Metricool's free plan has limits — for example, LinkedIn scheduling requires a paid plan.

Final verdict

Worth it for Instagram-first creators: Later. If the grid is your storefront and the visual planner is worth paying for, Starter at $18.75/month annual is a fair price for the best feed-preview tool under $20 — plus Linkin.bio. Audit your account count quarterly; the second-Instagram jump to Growth is how Later quietly doubles.

Worth it for everyone else: Metricool. At $20/month for 5 brands with unlimited users, deep analytics, ad tracking, and competitor data, it covers what most teams actually do — and its flat pricing doesn't punish you for stacking profiles on one platform. The free plan makes trying it free.

Skip Metricool if Instagram grid aesthetics are core to your brand — Later's visual planner is the tool for that job and Metricool has no answer for it. Skip Later if you run multiple accounts on one platform, need real analytics, or want teammates without multiplying the bill — the set model will tax all three.
