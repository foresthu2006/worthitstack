Every small business eventually faces the stack problem: a funnel builder here, an email tool there, a course platform somewhere else — five subscriptions, five logins, and a monthly bill that quietly passes $200. All-in-one marketing platforms promise to collapse that stack into one dashboard and one bill. The catch: they range from $0 to $499/month, and each is built for a different buyer. Pick the wrong one and you've bought a Ferrari to drive to the corner store — or a bicycle for a highway commute.

The five that matter in 2026: systeme.io (the budget all-in-one), GoHighLevel (the agency platform), Kajabi (the premium course brand), Kartra (the automation veteran), and ClickFunnels (the funnel specialist). Each gets an honest verdict below, matched to the buyer it actually serves.

Deep dive: each platform

1. systeme.io — best for solo creators on a budget

The value play that embarrasses everything else on price. The free plan covers 2,000 contacts, 3 funnels, 1 course, and unlimited email sending — a complete launch stack for $0. Paid tiers run $17 (Startup), $47 (Webinar, with evergreen webinars), and $97 (Unlimited) per month, all with zero platform transaction fees. Review sentiment is consistently strong (~4.8/5 across G2, Capterra, Trustpilot in 2026 roundups), with the praise centered on simplicity and the complaints on ceiling: no SMS, no real CRM pipelines, rules-based automation. If you're one person selling courses or coaching, start here and only leave when you outgrow it.

Try systeme.io free

2. GoHighLevel — best for agencies

The only platform on this list built for managing other people's marketing. Every client gets an isolated sub-account, white-labeled; you rebill SMS, email, and AI usage to their cards; and Agency Pro ($497/month) lets you resell the whole thing as your own SaaS. The CRM is real — visual pipelines, opportunity tracking, two-way SMS, missed-call text-back, review management. The costs are real too: $97–$497/month base plus metered usage (typically $20–$150/month extra), and a 2–4 week learning curve reviewers consistently flag. For agencies, the per-client economics are unbeatable; for solo operators, it's agency capacity you'll never use.

Try GoHighLevel

3. Kajabi — best for premium course brands

The prestige pick — and the price reflects it. Kajabi raised prices in January 2026 and eliminated its $89 Kickstarter entry plan; the cheapest way in is now Basic at $179/month ($143 annual), running to $499/month Pro. What you buy: the most polished course and coaching platform in the category, 0% platform transaction fees on every plan (via Kajabi Payments), and a brand that signals premium to high-ticket buyers. The contact limits are the squeeze — Basic dropped to 2,500 contacts — and affiliate programs, advanced automations, and API access are gated behind the $249/month Growth plan. Worth it for established coaches selling $2,000+ programs; absurd for a first course.

Visit Kajabi

4. Kartra — best for automation-heavy marketers

The veteran's choice. Kartra's behavioral automation — if/then sequences driven by page visits, video watch time, email engagement, and purchase behavior — remains the deepest on this list, and the feature set (funnels, email, memberships, helpdesk, affiliate management, calendars) is genuinely comprehensive. Pricing runs $59 (Essentials, 500 contacts, 5% transaction fee) to $549/month (Professional), with a 30-day free trial. The honest trade-off, consistent across 2026 reviews: the interface feels dated next to Kajabi and ClickFunnels, and the learning curve is the steepest here after GoHighLevel. Pick it when automation sophistication is the job, not when you want pretty.

Visit Kartra

5. ClickFunnels — best for funnel-obsessed sellers

The category-definer, still the best pure funnel builder. Startup ($97/month, ~$81 annual) and Pro ($297/month, ~$248 annual) both include unlimited funnels, pages, contacts, and courses — the 2024 repricing removed the old per-funnel limits entirely. The 14-day free trial is the standard test drive. What ClickFunnels doesn't do: native SMS, visual sales pipelines, reputation management, appointment scheduling — at $97/month, GoHighLevel includes all of that plus a real CRM. Buy ClickFunnels when funnels are the business and the Russell Brunson training ecosystem appeals; buy anything else when the funnel is one piece of a bigger operation.

The stack math: what "all-in-one" actually saves

The pitch is replacing 4–5 subscriptions. Here's what the replaced stack actually costs in 2026, so you can check the claim:

Typical separate stack: funnel builder (~$49/mo) + email platform (~$39/mo at 2,500 contacts) + course hosting (~$39–$119/mo) + scheduling tool (~$15/mo) + basic CRM (~$25/mo) = roughly $170–$250/month. Against that, systeme.io at $17–$47/month is a genuine 70–90% saving; GoHighLevel at $97–$297/month breaks even around the mid-range while adding agency infrastructure; Kajabi at $179–$499/month only makes sense when the premium brand and 0% fees move the revenue needle.

The honest caveat: all-in-ones are best-of-none. The email tool won't match Kit, the funnels won't match ClickFunnels' depth, the courses won't match Kajabi's polish. You buy consolidation and one bill, not best-in-class everything. If one function is your revenue engine, buy the specialist for that function and the all-in-one for the rest.

Frequently asked questions

Which all-in-one is cheapest?

systeme.io, by a wide margin: a genuinely usable free plan and paid tiers of $17–$97/month. The next cheapest entry is Kartra Essentials at $59/month (with a 5% transaction fee) or GoHighLevel/ClickFunnels at $97/month. Price alone shouldn't decide it, but systeme.io wins that race before features even enter the picture.

Can one platform really replace my whole stack?

For most solo businesses, yes — funnels, email, courses, and checkout are all covered. The functions that resist consolidation are advanced email automation (Kit still wins), deep analytics, and specialized tools like webinar platforms. Audit which tools you actually open before deciding.

Why is Kajabi so much more expensive?

Positioning and polish: Kajabi targets established creators selling premium programs, charges 0% platform fees, and invests in a premium brand experience. Its January 2026 price hike (and removal of the entry plan) pushed it further upmarket. You're paying for brand equity as much as software.

What's the catch with Kartra's Essentials plan?

The 5% transaction fee on every sale — at $2,000/month in revenue that's $100/month in fees on top of the $59 subscription. Upgrade to Starter ($119/month) removes it. Also: 500 contacts and 5 pages cap how far Essentials goes. That fee is the detail that changes the Essentials math fastest.

Should I start on systeme.io and upgrade to GoHighLevel later?

It's the most common lifecycle in this category: start free on systeme.io, grow into the $17–$47 tiers, and move to GoHighLevel when client work justifies $97–$297/month. Both support CSV imports; expect to rebuild funnels and automations manually. That staged path keeps early costs low without painting you into a corner.

Do any of these offer a free plan?

Only systeme.io has a permanent free plan worth using. Kartra offers a 30-day free trial, ClickFunnels a 14-day trial; GoHighLevel and Kajabi offer trials (14 days standard). Don't evaluate any of these on trial length alone — the pricing models differ too much.

systeme.io — free to start and $97/month max for unlimited everything, it's the easiest on-ramp of the five we compared. The natural upgrade path is GoHighLevel (from $97/mo) when agencies or client work enter the picture. Start where the learning curve is flattest; switch when client features become the bottleneck.

For funnel-first sellers, yes — at $97–$297/mo (Startup $97, Pro $297) it's still the best funnel builder, period. The catch: it's funnel-first, not a true all-in-one — email and course tooling cost extra. Everyone else gets more for less on systeme.io or GoHighLevel.

Which all-in-one platform is best for wedding planners or local service businesses?

GoHighLevel Starter at $97/month — the only all-in-one here with a real CRM for service businesses: visual pipelines, two-way SMS, missed-call text-back, appointment scheduling, and review management. systeme.io is cheaper (free to $97/month) but has no SMS and no real CRM pipelines. For follow-up-driven businesses, the communication stack is the product.

Final verdict

There's no single best all-in-one — there's the best one for your business model. systeme.io is the default recommendation for solo creators: start free, pay $17–$47/month, and you may never need anything else. GoHighLevel is the default for agencies: the sub-account architecture is infrastructure, not a feature. Kajabi is for established premium brands where the $179–$499/month buys positioning. Kartra is for automation nerds who'll trade polish for power. ClickFunnels is for funnel purists — best-in-class at one job, thinnest everywhere else.

The meta-advice: pick for where your business is now. The upgrade path from systeme.io to GoHighLevel is well-trodden, and every platform here offers a trial. The expensive mistake isn't picking the "wrong" tool — it's paying agency prices for solo needs, or premium prices for a first launch.
