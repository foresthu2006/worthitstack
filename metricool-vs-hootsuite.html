Metricool vs Hootsuite: Is the Incumbent Worth 5× in 2026?

This is the most lopsided comparison on this site — and that's exactly why it matters. Hootsuite is the incumbent all-in-one: scheduling plus a real unified inbox, social listening, and enterprise workflows, starting at $99 per user per month on annual billing. Metricool is the value challenger: scheduling plus ad tracking, competitor monitoring, and custom reports, starting at $20 per month flat — unlimited team members included.

The whole comparison turns on one structural difference: Hootsuite charges per seat, Metricool charges per brand bundle. Every person you add to Hootsuite multiplies the bill; every person you add to Metricool changes nothing. Here's the math, the real feature gaps, and the honest answer on whether the incumbent earns 5×.

Quick questions, quick answers

The questions people ask most about this comparison, answered directly. Stats and pricing verified October 2026.

How much does Hootsuite cost for a team?

A lot, fast. Standard is $99/user/month billed annually, scaling to $199 and $399/user/month — per-seat math that worsens with every hire. Metricool Starter is $25/month ($20 annual) flat for 5 brands with unlimited users, which is why small teams defect.

When is Hootsuite worth 5x the price of Metricool?

When social is a customer-service channel: you need the engagement inbox, social listening beyond your own profiles, or enterprise approval workflows and SSO. Metricool covers ~90% of what small teams use Hootsuite for at ~20% of the single-seat price — but inbox, listening, and approvals are Hootsuite-only.

Does Hootsuite have a free plan?

No — Hootsuite discontinued its free plan, which drew criticism from individual users. It offers a 30-day free trial instead. Metricool still has a free plan: 1 brand, 50 posts a month (LinkedIn scheduling requires a paid plan). If a permanent free tier matters, that gap alone settles the comparison.

What does Metricool have that Hootsuite doesn't?

Ad-platform tracking (Google Ads and TikTok Ads spend and performance), competitor benchmarking, a Looker Studio connector, and a genuinely free plan. Metricool also charges per brand with unlimited team members included, while Hootsuite bills $99+ per seat — so a 3-person team on Hootsuite starts at $297/month versus $20 on Metricool.

The quick verdict

Pick Hootsuite if social is a customer-service channel for you — you need a real engagement inbox with workflow automation, or social listening that goes beyond your own profiles, or enterprise approval workflows across a multi-person team.

Pick Metricool if you mainly need scheduling plus analytics with teeth. At $20/month for 5 brands with ad tracking and competitor data, it does ~90% of what small teams use Hootsuite for at ~20% of the single-seat price — and the per-seat math gets worse for Hootsuite with every hire.

The one-sentence version: Hootsuite is a customer-service and listening platform that also schedules; Metricool is a scheduling-and-analytics platform. Buy the one that matches the actual job.

The seat math that decides it

Three realistic scenarios, with the numbers (using annual pricing):

Scenario A: solopreneur, 6 profiles. Metricool Starter: $20/month — everything covered. Hootsuite Standard: $99/month — one seat, up to 10 accounts. Hootsuite costs 5× for the same job. Unless you need the inbox or listening, the incumbent loses on price alone.

Scenario B: 3-person marketing team, 12 profiles. Metricool Starter: still $20/month (unlimited users). Hootsuite Standard: 3 × $99 = $297/month ($3,564/year). And 12 profiles exceeds Standard's 10-account cap, so you're pushed toward Professional: 3 × $199 = $597/month — nearly 30× Metricool's Starter. This is where the per-seat model stops being a tool and starts being a line item.

Scenario C: freelancer managing 4 clients × 5 profiles (20 accounts). Metricool Advanced: $53/month for 15 brands (scaling to $159 for 50) with team approvals and branded reports. Hootsuite Professional: $199/month for a single seat with unlimited accounts — but add one assistant and it's $398. The incumbent's features are excellent; the seat meter just prices small teams out.

The honest takeaway: Hootsuite's pricing assumes the tool earns its keep as social-media infrastructure for a team with budget — listening, service workflows, enterprise reporting. If you only need scheduling plus good analytics, you're paying for infrastructure you won't use.

Listening and the engagement inbox

Hootsuite's real moat — and it's not scheduling. If your social channels are a customer-service desk, Hootsuite's unified inbox with automated replies is a different product from Metricool's thin engagement view. Add social listening — brand mentions, sentiment analysis, Talkwalker-powered intelligence on Enterprise — and you have software that replaces a reputation-monitoring tool you might be paying for separately. This is the only scenario where the $99+/seat price reads as a discount.

Metricool's counter — and where it lands blows. Ad-platform tracking is the standout: no Hootsuite tier below Enterprise ties your social scheduling to actual Google/TikTok ad spend. Competitor benchmarking and the Looker Studio connector also matter for reporting-heavy roles. But be honest about the gap: Metricool has no listening product, and its inbox won't run a service team.

Frequently asked questions

Is Hootsuite really worth 5 times Metricool?

For most small teams, no. Hootsuite Standard starts at $99/user/month on annual billing — five times Metricool's $20/month Starter — and the gap widens with every added seat since Hootsuite bills per user. Hootsuite earns its price only when its unified inbox, social listening, or enterprise workflows replace other software you're already paying for.

What does Hootsuite have that Metricool doesn't?

Three things: a real unified engagement inbox with workflow automation, full social listening (brand mentions, sentiment, Talkwalker-powered intelligence on higher tiers), and enterprise-grade team workflows — approvals, roles, and bulk scheduling up to 350 posts. Metricool's inbox is thin by comparison and it has no listening product.

What does Metricool have that Hootsuite doesn't?

Ad-platform tracking (Google Ads and TikTok Ads spend and performance), competitor benchmarking, a Looker Studio connector, and a genuinely free plan. Metricool still has a free plan: 1 brand, 50 posts a month (LinkedIn scheduling requires a paid plan). If a permanent free tier matters, that gap alone settles the comparison.

Which is better for agencies?

Metricool for most agencies: the Advanced plan ($53/month annual) covers 15 brands with team approvals and reports, scaling to 50 brands. Hootsuite's Professional ($199/user/month) only makes sense for agencies selling social listening or dedicated customer-service workflows — for pure scheduling-plus-reporting, the per-seat math destroys the margin.

Can I switch from Hootsuite to Metricool easily?

Scheduling-wise, yes: reconnect your social accounts via OAuth in Metricool and rebuild the queue — there is no one-click migration between schedulers. Historical analytics don't transfer, so export any reports you'll miss before canceling. Let Hootsuite's scheduled posts run out while you build the new queue.

Final verdict

Worth it for service-and-listening teams: Hootsuite. If your social channels field customer questions, or you need mentions, sentiment, and enterprise workflows, it's the most complete platform in the category — and the price is the cost of that completeness. Audit the list quarterly; the "only using the scheduler" trap is how $99+/seat bills quietly persist.

Worth it for everyone else: Metricool. At $20/month for 5 brands with ad tracking, competitor monitoring, and real reports, it covers what most small teams actually do with Hootsuite — at a fifth of one seat's price, with unlimited users included. The flat per-brand pricing is the whole argument.

Skip Metricool if you need a real engagement inbox or social listening — that's Hootsuite's home turf and Metricool can't fake it. Skip Hootsuite if you're a small team paying $99+ per seat just to schedule posts — you're buying infrastructure for a job you don't have.

Ready to decide?

Start with the free side: Metricool has a real free plan; Hootsuite has a 30-day trial. Test against your actual workflow, not the feature list.
