Why people switch from Fireflies.ai

People leave Fireflies.ai over three things: the metered AI-credit system for smart features like summaries and sentiment, the named bot that joins calls as an attendee (awkward on client calls), and paying for CRM and deal-intelligence sales features they never use:

  • The AI-credit system. Fireflies gives you unlimited transcription, but the smart features — AI summaries, topic tracking, sentiment — consume AI credits that are metered per plan. Heavy users run into this.
  • The bot. Fireflies joins your calls as a named attendee. Some teams find that awkward on client calls — and some guests ask it to leave.
  • You don't need the sales features. If you just want notes and summaries, you're paying for CRM automation and deal intelligence you'll never touch.

The 7 alternatives, reviewed

1. Fathom — the best free alternative

Fathom's free plan is almost unfair to competitors: unlimited recording, unlimited transcription, and AI summaries with no minute cap on individual accounts. Paid plans (~$19/user/mo annual) add CRM sync, call scoring, and team features. If your only complaint about Fireflies is the price, this is your answer.

The catch: the integration depth isn't Fireflies-level — CRM automation and custom workflows are thinner. Heavy Salesforce users will miss it.

2. Otter.ai — the all-rounder

Otter is the longest-running name in meeting transcription: real-time captions, a proper mobile app, and in-person recording that none of the bot-based tools do well. Free covers 300 minutes a month; Pro is ~$16.99/user/mo billed annually. Better for general teams than sales teams.

The catch: no deal intelligence, no talk-time analytics, and minute caps instead of Fireflies-style unlimited transcription. See our full Fireflies.ai vs Otter.ai breakdown.

3. tl;dv — the European pick

tl;dv (too long; didn't view) records Zoom, Meet, and Teams with AI summaries and topic tagging, and leans hard into GDPR compliance — EU data hosting, which matters for privacy-sensitive European teams. Free tier is limited; Pro runs ~$29/user/mo.

The catch: pricier than Fireflies for what most small teams need, and the integration list is shorter.

4. Granola — no bot in your calls

Granola's pitch is simple: it records from your own device instead of joining as a meeting bot, so there's no "Fred is recording this meeting" attendee to explain to clients. The AI notes pull from your existing notes and documents for context. Paid plans start around $10/mo.

The catch: it only records what your device hears — no-shows on meetings you didn't attend, and no team-wide automation. It's a personal tool, not a sales platform.

5. Avoma — Fireflies with pipeline brains

Avoma bundles meeting transcription with conversation intelligence and pipeline management: deal scoring, activity tracking, and coaching insights. Plans start around $19/user/mo. It's the mid-market answer for teams who want Gong-style insight without Gong-style pricing.

The catch: transcription quality reviews are mixed next to the specialists, and at ~2x Fireflies' price it's only worth it if you'll use the pipeline features.

6. Gong — the enterprise standard

Gong invented the revenue-intelligence category: every call analyzed against win patterns, rep coaching, forecasting signal, deep CRM integration. There's no public pricing and no self-serve plan — you're negotiating an enterprise contract (commonly reported in the low hundreds per user per month).

The catch: it's not a Fireflies replacement for normal teams — it's a different planet of price and implementation. Only relevant if "meeting assistant" is a company-wide sales initiative.

7. Fireflies.ai itself — don't switch without reading this

Honest take: half the people researching "Fireflies alternatives" should just stay. Unlimited transcription at ~$10/seat/mo, the deepest CRM automation in its price class, and a genuinely free tier to start — none of the free options match its workflow depth for sales teams.

FAQ

What is the best free alternative to Fireflies.ai?

Fathom's free plan is the strongest free option — unlimited recording, transcription, and summaries with no minute cap on individual accounts. Otter.ai's free plan is also genuinely usable at 300 minutes per month. Fireflies itself has a free tier, but it's limited compared to its paid plans.

What is the best Fireflies alternative for sales teams?

Gong is the enterprise standard for sales call analysis, with deal-intelligence features Fireflies can't match — but it costs roughly 10x more. Avoma sits in the middle, with pipeline analytics and conversation scoring at a fraction of Gong's price. For small sales teams on a budget, Fireflies' own Pro plan with its CRM integrations is enough.

Which meeting assistant doesn't send a bot to your calls?

Granola takes a different approach: it captures audio on your device instead of joining as a meeting bot, so no 'bot' ever appears in the attendee list. Fathom also joins as a bot on some plans but can record from your device in certain setups. Most transcription-first tools, including Fireflies and Otter, join as a named bot.

Is Fireflies.ai worth it, or should I switch?

For sales and revenue teams, yes — it's still the best value at ~$10/seat/mo, with the deepest CRM integrations. Switch only for a specific need: Fathom if you only want meeting notes, Otter for in-person capture, or Granola for bot-free recording. There's no better general-purpose deal for a sales team in 2026.

Can you use Fireflies.ai and Otter.ai together?

Technically yes, but there's little reason to — you'd pay twice for overlapping transcription. A more common stack is a free tool (Fathom or Otter free) for everyday notes plus a sales-specific tool (Fireflies or Gong) for pipeline calls. Two paid transcribers on one calendar is the costliest way to get the same notes.

Which Fireflies alternative is best for European teams?

tl;dv is the best pick for European teams thanks to its GDPR focus, starting around $29 per user per month with a limited free plan. Fathom is the stronger free option overall (free tier with unlimited meetings, around $19 per user per month paid), but tl;dv is the purpose-built choice for EU-based teams.

How much does Fireflies.ai actually cost per user?

The entry price is around $10/seat/month with a limited free plan — but the headline hides the metered AI-credit system. Unlimited transcription is included; the smart features (AI summaries, topic tracking, sentiment) consume AI credits metered per plan, and heavy users run into the cap. Budget for your summary volume, not just the seat price.

Which meeting assistant has the best free plan?

Fathom — its free tier covers unlimited meetings, the most generous free plan in this category, with paid plans from around $19/user/month. Otter.ai's free plan is capped at 300 minutes per month, Fireflies.ai's is limited, and Granola's free tier is also limited — so Fathom wins for anyone who lives on the free tier.
