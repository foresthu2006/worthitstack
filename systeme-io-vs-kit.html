Kit (formerly ConvertKit — renamed in 2024, same product) is the email platform built for creators: visual automations, best-in-class deliverability, and a free plan that covers up to 10,000 subscribers. Systeme.io is the all-in-one challenger: email plus funnels, courses, and checkout, starting free and topping out at $97/month for unlimited everything. If you're a creator who sells things, this comparison is genuinely hard — because Kit's paid plans cost more than systeme.io's entire top tier.

The honest frame: Kit is email done properly; systeme.io is the whole business stack, cheaply. One of those is worth more than the other depending on where your revenue actually comes from. Here's the full breakdown.

The creator math at three list sizes

Scenario A: new creator, 800 subscribers, selling a first mini-course. Kit: free plan covers everything except sequences ($0). systeme.io: free plan covers 2,000 contacts plus the course and funnels ($0). Both are free — but only systeme.io hosts the course and checkout. If you're selling, systeme.io's free tier is the complete business; Kit's free tier is the complete newsletter.

Scenario B: newsletter-first creator, 3,000 subscribers, no products yet. Kit Creator: $59/month with the best automation builder in the creator space. systeme.io Startup: $17/month with unlimited emails — but the email tooling is simpler and the deliverability reputation isn't Kit's. If email revenue is the business, Kit's $42/month premium buys demonstrably better tooling; if you're building toward selling, systeme.io keeps the door open.

Scenario C: established creator, 10,000 subscribers, selling courses. Kit Creator: ~$139/month for email alone — plus a course platform ($39–$179/month elsewhere), plus a funnel builder. Realistic stack: $200+/month. systeme.io Webinar: $47/month for email, funnels, courses, and webinars combined. At this size the math isn't close — unless your email list is your #1 revenue driver, in which case Kit's automation quality can pay for itself in conversion rate.

The pattern: Kit's pricing scales with your success (which stings precisely when revenue arrives); systeme.io's pricing scales with platform tiers (which stays flat as your list grows). Newsletter-first businesses outgrow systeme.io's email depth; product businesses outgrow Kit's pricing.

Email quality vs stack breadth

Kit's edge — and it's the whole product. Kit's visual automation builder is consistently rated the easiest serious automation tool in email marketing, its deliverability tests at the top of creator-focused tools, and features like the Creator Network (newsletter cross-promotion) and built-in paid subscriptions are genuine growth levers for publishers. The free plan — 10,000 subscribers, unlimited emails — is the most generous in email marketing. For a writer or podcaster whose business is the list, this is the professional choice.

systeme.io's edge — and it's the whole stack. Everything Kit doesn't do, systeme.io does for $17/month: funnels, checkout, course hosting, evergreen webinars, affiliate management. The email tool is simpler — rules instead of visual workflows, no subscriber scoring — but it's unlimited on every plan and perfectly adequate for launches, welcome sequences, and course drips. For a creator who sells things, the question isn't "is the email better" but "is the email $120/month better than having funnels and courses included."

Frequently asked questions

Is Kit still called ConvertKit?

Kit rebranded from ConvertKit in 2024 — same company, same product, expanded scope (commerce, sponsorships, creator profiles). Older comparisons and affiliate content still say “ConvertKit,” while the pricing page now says Kit. Treat the two names as interchangeable when you're reading reviews written before the rebrand.

Can I really use Kit free with 10,000 subscribers?

Yes — the Newsletter plan covers up to 10,000 subscribers with unlimited emails, forms, and landing pages, no credit card required. The real limits: one basic automation and no sequences until you pay. It's the most generous free tier in email marketing.

Which is better for selling an online course?

systeme.io — it hosts the course, builds the sales funnel, and processes checkout in the same $17–$47/month plan. Kit handles the email marketing well but needs a separate course platform, which is where the stack cost doubles. One bill versus two integrations is the deciding factor for most course creators.

Does systeme.io's email deliver as well as Kit's?

Kit has the stronger deliverability reputation in 2026 tests and reviews — it's a core differentiator the company invests in. systeme.io's deliverability is reported as solid but not best-in-class. If inbox placement is your revenue lever, that matters; for launches and course drips, most users won't feel the difference.

Can I migrate from Kit to systeme.io later (or vice versa)?

Both support CSV contact imports, and Kit advertises free migrations on paid plans; systeme.io advertises migration assistance on its Unlimited plan. Expect to rebuild automations manually — sequences don't transfer between platforms. Budget a weekend for a simple list, longer for complex workflows.

Why does Kit get so expensive as my list grows?

Kit prices by subscriber count — Creator runs $39/month at 1,000 subs, ~$89 at 5,000, ~$139 at 10,000. It's the standard email-platform model, and it means your bill grows with your success. Systeme.io's flat tier pricing ($17–$97/month) is the structural alternative — the trade-off is simpler email tooling.
