Metricool's pitch is the friendliest billing model in social media management: you pay per brand, not per user and not per channel, with unlimited teammates on every paid plan. A brand is one set of connected accounts — one Instagram, one Facebook, one TikTok, one LinkedIn. The free plan is real (1 brand, 50 posts a month), and Starter's $25/mo covers 5 brands with ad tracking, competitor benchmarking, and client-ready reports. People land here for the same reason every time: the billing unit is honest, but it has edges — two Instagram accounts for the same company count as two brands, X (Twitter) costs extra on every tier, and LinkedIn scheduling requires the Starter plan.

Why people switch from Metricool

People leave Metricool over five friction points: the paid X add-on, LinkedIn scheduling gated behind the $25/mo Starter plan, brand counting that can jump agencies a tier overnight, a thin engagement inbox vs Hootsuite or Sprout Social, and lighter AI features than Buffer or Later:

  • The X (Twitter) add-on. X charges platforms high API fees, and Metricool passes that through as a paid add-on from the Starter plan up. If X is a core distribution channel for you, the add-on stings — and some rivals bundle it.
  • LinkedIn scheduling is gated. The free plan doesn't cover LinkedIn scheduling; you need Starter ($25/mo) to schedule to it. Buffer's free plan handles three channels including LinkedIn.
  • Brand counting gets you. Two Instagram accounts for the same company count as two brands. Agencies that onboard a client's second account can jump a pricing tier overnight — the ladder is fair but the math surprises people.
  • The engagement inbox is thin. Metricool schedules and analyzes beautifully, but the reply-to-comments-and-DMs workflow is light compared to Hootsuite or Sprout Social. If social is a customer service channel, this is a real gap.
  • AI features are lighter. Buffer's AI assistant drafts captions, repurposes articles into posts, and generates ideas on every plan; Later's visual planning is tuned for creators. Metricool's automation leans toward analytics, not creation.

The 7 alternatives, reviewed

1. Buffer — the simplest alternative

Buffer is the scheduler everything else gets measured against for simplicity: a queue, a calendar, done. The free plan covers 3 channels with 10 scheduled posts each — and includes the AI Assistant, which drafts captions and repurposes articles into social snippets. Essentials is $5 per channel per month on annual billing, so a solo creator with Instagram, TikTok, and LinkedIn pays $0 on free or $15/mo on paid — cheaper than Metricool's Starter with zero fuss. Compare with Metricool free

The catch: analytics are shallow, and channel-count pricing punishes multi-brand setups — 10 channels cost $50/mo on Essentials, where Metricool's flat $25 Starter covers 5 whole brands. Buffer wins under ~4 channels and loses past it. See our full Metricool vs Buffer breakdown.

2. Later — the visual-creator pick

Later is built around Instagram-first visual planning: drag-and-drop feed preview, Linkin.bio, and a media library that works the way creators think. Starter is $25/mo ($18.75/mo on annual billing) for one social set covering 8 profiles with 30 posts per profile per month — competitive with Metricool's Starter when your workflow is visual.

The catch: analytics are thinner than Metricool's, and X is not on its supported-platform list — if X distribution matters to you, this is a non-starter. Growth ($50/mo) is where the 16-profile capacity unlocks.

3. Hootsuite — the engagement pick

Hootsuite is the all-in-one incumbent: scheduling plus a real unified inbox, social listening, and competitive intelligence. Standard starts around $99/user/month on annual billing (1 user, 10 social accounts) — more than double Metricool's 5-brand Starter — and it earns that price only when the inbox, listening, and reporting replace other software you were buying separately.

The catch: the price. For a solo brand, paying $99/mo for inbox features you don't use is a bad trade, and the per-user model punishes teams. Metricool's unlimited teammates on every paid plan look generous by comparison.

4. Sendible — the agency pick

Sendible is built for agencies that live in client reports: white-label dashboards and reports, per-plan pricing from around $30/mo, and unlimited users. If Metricool's brand-ladder math is getting you at 10+ brands and you need client-facing branding, Sendible's agency tooling is the direct upgrade.

The catch: the base plan is entry-level capacity, and advanced features stack up quickly as you scale. Agencies should price their actual client count, not the headline.

5. SocialPilot — the budget per-plan pick

SocialPilot's pitch is simple: lots of profiles for not much money — around $25.50/mo on per-plan pricing with 14-day trial. It's the flat-rate budget play for small teams that want Metricool-style per-plan billing at the lowest sticker price in the mid-tier.

The catch: reviewers consistently rate the UX and analytics depth a step below Metricool's. The bill is smaller, and the product shows why.

6. Sprout Social — the enterprise pick

Sprout Social is the tool for teams that treat social as a customer service channel: Standard is $249/mo (1 user, 5 profiles) with a 30-day trial, Professional $399 (3 users, 10 profiles), Advanced $499 (5 users, 10 profiles with advanced reporting). Social listening, case management, and deep analytics are genuinely best in class.

The catch: it's 5–10x the price of Metricool with no free tier. Unless social customer care or compliance reporting is the job, this is far more tool than most small teams need.

7. Metricool itself — don't switch without reading this

Honest take: if you manage multiple brands and want real analytics, half the people researching "Metricool alternatives" should just stay. The per-brand model with unlimited teammates is the fairest pricing in the category once you're past ~5 profiles, the free plan is genuinely usable, ad tracking and competitor monitoring have no equal at this price, and the downloadable reports are client-ready. Should I choose Metricool or Buffer?

Buffer for solo creators with a few accounts: simpler, cheaper at small counts (free or $5/channel annual). Metricool once you manage 5+ profiles or care about analytics — Starter's $25/mo covers 5 brands with ad tracking, competitor monitoring, and reports Buffer can't match at any tier.

If social is a customer-service channel for you rather than a broadcast channel, Hootsuite is the pick on this list.
