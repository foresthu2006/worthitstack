The 7 alternatives, reviewed

1. Kit — the creator pick

Kit (formerly ConvertKit) is the tool newsletter-first creators reach for: free for up to 10,000 subscribers, landing pages, forms, and digital-product selling built for the creator workflow. Paid plans from $29/month add automations and reporting. It doesn't do funnels or courses the way Systeme.io does — but if email is your business, nothing is smoother.

The catch: it's email-first, not all-in-one. You'll need other tools for funnels, webinars, or a real course platform. See our full Systeme.io vs Kit breakdown.

2. GoHighLevel — the agency pick

GoHighLevel is the only tool here built for managing other people's marketing: client sub-accounts, full white-labeling, CRM pipelines, and SaaS-mode reselling at $97/month. For agencies it's not an alternative to Systeme.io so much as a different product category — Systeme.io simply can't do any of this.

The catch: $97/month with no free plan is a commitment, and it's overkill if you're only running your own business. See our full GoHighLevel vs Systeme.io comparison.

3. Kajabi — the premium course platform

Kajabi is what course sellers graduate to: a polished branded student experience, coaching products, community, and podcasts alongside courses. Plans start at $149/month on annual billing. If you charge premium prices, the platform's polish pays for itself in perceived value.

The catch: it's 5–10x the price of Systeme.io for comparable email and funnel features — you're paying for course polish and brand, not raw capability.

4. ClickFunnels — the original funnel builder

ClickFunnels invented the funnel-builder category and still has the biggest template marketplace and community. Plans start at $97/month — roughly 5x Systeme.io's entry tier. Systeme.io even runs a free migration service to pull ClickFunnels users over, which tells you who wins on price.

The catch: you're paying for the ecosystem and templates, not more capability. For most solopreneurs, it's legacy pricing for equivalent funnels.

5. MailerLite — the cheapest serious email

If all you need from the "all-in-one" is email, MailerLite is the budget answer: free up to 1,000 subscribers, a better drag-and-drop editor than Systeme.io's, and paid plans from $10/month. It won't build funnels or host courses — it's the pick for people who realize they never needed the all-in-one.

The catch: email only. Landing pages exist but they're basic; there's no funnel builder, automation is lighter, and you'll bolt on everything else.

6. GetResponse — email plus webinars

GetResponse sits between email tools and all-in-ones: solid email marketing, landing pages, webinars (its historical strength), and a free plan up to 500 contacts. Paid plans start around $19/month. It's the pick if webinars are core to your selling and you want them under the same roof as email.

The catch: the funnel and course features are thinner than Systeme.io's, and the interface shows its age. See our full GetResponse vs Mailchimp comparison.

7. Systeme.io itself — don't switch without reading this

Honest take: half the people researching "Systeme.io alternatives" should just stay. A real free plan (2,000 contacts, 3 funnels, blog, email), full all-in-one at $17/month, free migration from ClickFunnels, and consistent review sentiment that it's the best value in the category. No alternative matches its price-to-capability ratio for solopreneurs.
