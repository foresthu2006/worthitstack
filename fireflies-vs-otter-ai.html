Fireflies.ai vs Otter.ai: Which Is Worth It in 2026?

In short: Pick Otter.ai for team meetings — reliable transcription, 300 free minutes a month, a mobile app, and in-person recording. Pick Fireflies.ai for sales calls — unlimited transcription, talk ratios, sentiment tracking, and notes pushed into HubSpot or Salesforce. Fireflies.ai and Otter.ai are the two meeting assistants everyone cross-shops, and the comparison looks deceptively simple: both join your Zoom, Meet, or Teams calls, transcribe everything, and hand you AI summaries. But they were built for different jobs. Otter.ai is the all-rounder for general teams — real-time transcription, a mobile app, even in-person recording, with per-user minute limits that scale up cleanly. Fireflies.ai is the revenue-team specialist — deal intelligence, talk-time analytics, and 100+ integrations that push meeting outcomes into your CRM automatically, with unlimited transcription but a separate AI-credit system for the smart features.

The decision comes down to one question: are your meetings mostly conversations to remember, or sales calls to act on? Here's the full breakdown.

Two details that change the math. First, Fireflies' headline prices don't include AI usage: summaries, action items, and AskFred consume AI credits (20/month on Pro, 30/month on Business), and heavy AI users buy top-ups. 2026 pricing analyses flag this as the most under-discussed cost in the category. Second, Otter's free tier caps conversations at 30 minutes — a normal hour-long meeting gets cut off, which makes the free plan a trial for real teams rather than a permanent solution.

Fireflies' credit system, explained

This deserves its own section because it's the #1 source of billing surprise. On every Fireflies plan — including paid ones — advanced AI features (AI summaries beyond the allowance, AskFred queries, Smart Highlights, custom AI skills) consume AI credits from a monthly pool. Pro gets 20/month, Business 30/month. Transcription itself is unlimited; the credits govern the intelligence layer on top.

For a salesperson who asks Fred to summarize every call and pull action items, 20–30 credits can run out before month-end. Top-ups are available, but they turn a $19/user/month line item into a variable one. If your team will lean hard on AI summaries, model this before committing — or compare against Otter's fixed minute allowance, which at least fails loudly and predictably when you hit it.

Meeting intelligence vs transcription

Fireflies' edge — and it's the reason sales teams pick it. Beyond transcription, Fireflies analyzes how the meeting went: who talked how much, whether sentiment shifted, when competitors got mentioned, which topics consumed the most time. For a sales manager coaching five reps, that's a performance dataset, not a notes tool. The workflow automation completes the picture — notes, tasks, and CRM updates happen without anyone opening HubSpot. Reviewers consistently describe the setup as heavier than Otter's, but the payoff lands for teams where calls are revenue.

Otter's edge — and why general teams default to it. Otter is the simpler, broader tool: live transcription you can watch during the call, a genuinely good mobile app, and in-person recording that covers conference rooms and hallway conversations no bot can join. It's the meeting assistant for the whole company, not just the sales floor. The trade-offs are the minute meters — 300 minutes on free, 1,200 on Pro — and the absence of Fireflies' analytics layer. If your heaviest meeting user is in product or operations, not sales, those meters are the more honest constraint.

Quick answers

Is Fireflies or Otter cheaper for heavy meeting loads?

Fireflies is cheaper once you record a lot. Otter meters transcription minutes (Pro caps at 1,200/month; $16.99/user monthly or $8.33 annual), while Fireflies Pro is $18 monthly or $10 annual with unlimited transcription. Past ~20 recorded hours a month, Fireflies wins; lighter users pay less with Otter. Note Fireflies AI credits are a separate monthly pool (20 on Pro, 30 on Business).

Which is better for sales teams — Fireflies or Otter?

Fireflies. It tracks talk ratios, sentiment shifts, and competitor mentions, and pushes notes and tasks into HubSpot or Salesforce automatically — a sales-coaching dataset, not just notes. Otter is built for everyday transcription: a mobile app, in-person recording, and live captions, with no sales-intelligence layer.

Does Fireflies' free plan beat Otter's?

For raw volume, yes. Fireflies' free tier includes unlimited transcription (with limited AI summaries and storage); Otter's free tier gives 300 minutes a month with 30-minute conversation caps. Occasional recorders are fine on Otter free; heavy recorders who don't need full AI features get more from Fireflies free.

Frequently asked questions

Is Fireflies.ai really unlimited?

Transcription is unlimited on all plans, but the AI layer isn't: summaries, AskFred, and smart highlights consume monthly AI credits (20 on Pro, 30 on Business), and storage is metered on Free and Pro. It's unlimited recording, metered intelligence — budget the AI credits against how many summaries you'll actually read.

Can I use Otter.ai free forever?

For light use, yes — 300 minutes/month with real-time transcription is genuinely usable. But the 30-minute conversation cap cuts off normal hour-long meetings, and file imports are limited to 3 lifetime on the free plan. Heavy users land on Pro ($16.99/month) fast.

Do attendees know they're being recorded?

Yes — both join as visible bots (Fireflies as "Fireflies.ai Notetaker," Otter as OtterPilot) and announce themselves. Recording consent laws vary by jurisdiction: disclose recording at the start of every call regardless, and check your company's policy before enabling auto-join.

Which integrates better with Salesforce?

Fireflies — it's the deeper integration, with automatic note and task pushing plus conversation intelligence mapped to deal records. Otter offers Salesforce and HubSpot connectors on Pro and up, but the automation depth is shallower. If Salesforce is where your deals live, Fireflies' automatic syncing saves your reps the manual logging Otter still requires.

What happens when I run out of Fireflies AI credits?

Transcription and recording continue — only AI features (summaries, AskFred, smart highlights) pause until credits reset or you buy top-ups. It's a softer failure than hitting Otter's minute cap, but it can still leave a sales team without summaries mid-month.

Which is better for non-sales teams?

Otter.ai. Product, operations, and leadership meetings need reliable transcription and search, not talk-ratio analytics — and Otter's mobile app plus in-person recording cover meetings that Fireflies' bot model can't reach. Sales teams get more from Fireflies' coaching metrics; everyone else should default to Otter.
