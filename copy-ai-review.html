The pivot: what Copy.ai is now

Copy.ai's current product has two faces:

  • Copy.ai Chat — the accessible tier: an AI chat interface with brand voice, templates, and workflow access for small teams.
  • The GTM platform — the strategic bet: no-code AI workflows ("agents") that automate go-to-market operations — prospecting lists, lead enrichment, personalized outbound, CRM updates, content operations at scale.

Reviewers in 2026 consistently describe the same split personality: the chat product competes with ChatGPT/Claude for writing tasks, while the platform competes with sales-automation tools like Clay, Apollo, and custom Zapier/Make stacks. The company's energy — and pricing — has clearly moved toward the platform side. If you're evaluating Copy.ai, decide which product you're actually buying, because they're priced and positioned like different companies.
