The workspace math that decides it

Sendible's billing unit is the workspace: 6 social profiles in one bundle. Extra workspaces bill on top at $35/month each on Core, $30 on Plus, $25 on Premium, $20 on Elite, $15 on Enterprise. There is no way to buy a single extra profile — you buy them six at a time.

Do the math for a typical small agency client roster: three clients, each with Instagram, Facebook, and TikTok = 9 profiles. On Sendible Core ($35, 6 profiles) you need a second workspace: $70/month for 9 profiles, three of them empty. On Metricool Starter ($20 annual, 5 brands), three clients = 3 brand slots with room to spare: $20/month.

Flip it to a setup Sendible was designed for: one client with exactly six profiles. Now Sendible Core's $29.75/month annual is a fair price for 6 profiles with agency workflows — while Metricool's 5-brand Starter still costs $20. The gap narrows when your profile counts land exactly on multiples of six, and it explodes when they don't.

The step-up comparison is uglier at the low end: growing from 5 to 6 brands on Metricool means jumping Starter to the 10-brand tier ($25 → $45/month monthly, $20 → $36 annual). Growing from 6 to 7 profiles on Sendible Core means a second full workspace: $35 → $70. Sendible's growth jump is nearly twice as expensive — that's the workspace trap in one number.

Sendible's agency moat

Strip away pricing and Sendible is the better agency operations tool — that was its whole design brief. Client dashboards keep each client's profiles, calendar, and reports isolated. Approval workflows route content to clients for sign-off before it publishes. The priority inbox and content discovery features are built for teams managing other people's brands, not their own.

But the moat has a moat. White-label reporting — branded reports with your logo — is a paid add-on available only from Elite, at $349/month on Elite ($299) and $800/month on Enterprise ($750). That puts branded client reporting at roughly $648/month minimum. If you came to Sendible expecting white-label agency reporting at agency prices, check this line item before you commit — it's the most common post-purchase surprise in Sendible reviews.

Metricool's counterpunch

Metricool isn't trying to be an agency operations tool — it's an analytics platform that happens to schedule. Website analytics, Meta and Google ad spend tracking, and competitor analysis live in the same dashboard as your calendar, at prices where Sendible's equivalent would need two tiers higher. The free plan is real (1 brand, 50 posts a month), and the unlimited-users-on-every-tier policy means your fifth teammate costs you nothing.

The catches are honest ones: X (Twitter) posting is a paid add-on on every tier, LinkedIn scheduling requires a paid plan, and Metricool has no client-isolation concept — if you manage other people's brands and need sign-off workflows, you're duct-taping it with team approvals on Advanced.

Who should pick Metricool, and who should pick Sendible?

Pick Metricool if you're a business, creator, or small agency doing your own publishing — $20/month annual for 5 brands with deep analytics and ad tracking. Pick Sendible if you're an agency isolating real clients: client dashboards, approval workflows, and profile bundling at $35/month for 6 profiles with unlimited users.

Pick Metricool if…

  • You're publishing for your own business or clients, not managing client accounts
  • Analytics, ad tracking, or competitor data matter
  • You want a real free plan to start on
  • Your profile counts don't land on multiples of six
  • White-label-style reporting at Advanced prices beats Sendible's $648 minimum

Pick Sendible if…

  • You run an agency with real clients to isolate and invoice
  • Client approval workflows are non-negotiable
  • Each client has ~6 profiles (the workspace unit fits naturally)
  • Unlimited users matter more than per-profile cost
  • You need priority inbox + content discovery in one tool

Frequently asked questions

Is Sendible cheaper than Metricool?

No at the entry tier. Sendible Core is $35/month ($29.75/month annual) for 6 profiles, versus Metricool Starter at $25/month ($20/month annual) for 5 brands. Metricool has a free plan (1 brand, 50 posts a month); Sendible offers only a 14-day trial. At the low end you pay more for agency plumbing, not scheduling volume.

What is a Sendible workspace?

A workspace is Sendible's billing unit: 6 social profiles tied to one client workspace. Core includes 1 workspace; Plus 3; Premium 7; Elite 15; Enterprise 50. Extra workspaces bill on top — $35/month each on Core, $30 on Plus, $25 on Premium, $20 on Elite, $15 on Enterprise. On Core, a 7th profile triggers a second workspace.

Does Sendible have a free plan?

No permanent free plan as of 2026 — Sendible offers a 14-day free trial on paid plans, and that's it. Metricool still has a real free plan: 1 brand, 50 posts a month (LinkedIn scheduling requires a paid plan). If you want to park somewhere free while you grow, Metricool is the only one of the two that lets you.

Why did Sendible change its pricing in 2026?

Sendible retired the old Creator, Traction, Scale, and Advanced plans and replaced them with five workspace-based tiers (Core, Plus, Premium, Elite, Enterprise), dropping per-seat pricing entirely — every plan now includes unlimited users, so the bill follows profiles and workspaces instead of headcount. If a comparison quotes a $29 Creator plan, it's describing the retired structure.

Which is better for agencies: Metricool or Sendible?

Sendible, if you need real agency plumbing: client dashboards, approval workflows, and client isolation. Metricool Advanced ($67/month; $53/month annual; 15 brands, unlimited users) is the cheaper per-client scheduling bet. The split: Sendible for client management, Metricool for cheap multi-client publishing — though Sendible's white-label reporting is a paid add-on from Elite ($349/month).

What happens when I add a 7th social profile on Sendible Core?

Sendible Core includes 6 profiles in 1 workspace, so a 7th requires a second workspace — your $35 bill becomes $70 for one profile. On Metricool, growing from 5 to 6 brands means stepping from the Starter to the 10-brand tier. Both jump at their limits; Sendible's jump is twice as expensive because workspaces only sell in packs of six.

Final verdict

Worth it for solo publishers and small teams: Metricool. At $20/month for 5 brands with unlimited users, deep analytics, ad tracking, and a real free plan, it covers what most teams actually do — and its flat per-brand pricing doesn't punish you for an odd number of profiles.

Worth it for real agencies: Sendible. Client dashboards, approval workflows, and workspace-per-client isolation starting at $35/month ($29.75 annual) are genuinely worth paying for when clients are the business. Unlimited users on every tier means the whole team is on board at no extra seat cost.

Skip Sendible if you don't manage clients — you're paying agency prices for agency plumbing you'll never use, and the workspace-of-six model taxes uneven profile counts. Skip Metricool if client isolation, approval sign-off, and priority inbox are core to your workflow — Metricool's team approvals on Advanced are a workaround, not a system.
