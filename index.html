Short answers to the questions buyers ask first

Direct answers, priced and sourced on the pages they come from. Each answer below is the same answer published in that page's FAQ — no separate homepage verdicts.

What is the best email marketing tool for small businesses in 2026?

GetResponse is our best overall pick — it has the deepest automation per dollar of any tool we compared, and its per-contact scaling stays gentle as your list grows. Creators who live on newsletters should look at Kit instead; MailerLite wins the free tier.

Full answer and pricing →

What's the best free AI meeting assistant?

Fathom — its free tier includes unlimited recording and transcription, which is more generous than any competitor's free plan. Otter.ai's free tier (300 minutes per month) is the best second choice for light users. Both work without a credit card, so the cheapest way to test is to run Fathom on your next two meetings.

Full answer and pricing →

What's the best free social media scheduling tool?

Buffer — 3 channels free with no credit card is the most usable free scheduling tier. Metricool also offers a free plan. Later's free plan was discontinued (14-day trial on paid plans only), per 2026 reviews. Free-tier caveat: analytics depth is what the paid plans buy; if you're choosing on reporting, Metricool's free tier tells you more.

Full answer and pricing →

What's the best free AI video editor?

CapCut — it's genuinely free with auto-captions, effects, and templates, not a crippled trial. Runway, Descript, HeyGen, Opus Clip, and VEED all offer limited free tiers worth testing, but CapCut is the only full editor that's free. Free full editing: start CapCut. Specialist tools: test their free tiers when a specific job appears.

Full answer and pricing →

Which all-in-one is cheapest?

systeme.io, by a wide margin: a genuinely usable free plan and paid tiers of $17–$97/month. The next cheapest entry is Kartra Essentials at $59/month (with a 5% transaction fee) or GoHighLevel/ClickFunnels at $97/month. Price alone shouldn't decide it, but systeme.io wins that race before features even enter the picture.

Full answer and pricing →

What's the best free chatbot for a small business website?

Tidio — 50 conversations/month free with the Lyro AI agent included is enough for most small sites. Chatbase and Crisp also offer limited free tiers; ManyChat is free up to 1,000 contacts for social DMs. Start on Tidio's free tier to test whether chat converts your visitors; only pay once the conversation volume justifies a plan.

Full answer and pricing →

FAQ

What is Worth It Stack?

Worth It Stack is an independent comparison site for AI software and SaaS tools aimed at small businesses and creators. Every page shows real pricing, feature breakdowns, and a clear verdict — who should buy, and who shouldn't. No hype, and no fake claims of hands-on testing we didn't do.

How does Worth It Stack make money?

Through affiliate commissions: when you sign up for a tool through one of our links — Systeme.io, GoHighLevel, Fireflies.ai, HeyGen, Synthesia, or Metricool — we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. Commissions don't change what we recommend; our full disclosure page explains the model in detail.

How do you verify pricing?

We check each vendor's official pricing page and re-verify comparisons regularly — most pages were last checked in October 2026. Pricing is converted to plain monthly and annual terms so tiers compare cleanly. When a vendor restructures plans, we correct the affected pages rather than guessing.
