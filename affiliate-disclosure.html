Affiliate Disclosure

Last updated October 2026

The short version: Worth It Stack participates in affiliate programs. When you click a link on this site and make a purchase, we may earn a commission — at no additional cost to you.

In short: Many links on Worth It Stack are affiliate links — if you buy through them, the vendor pays us a commission at no extra cost to you. You pay exactly the vendor’s price; the commission comes from their margin. Commissions never decide our verdicts.

Quick questions, quick answers

The questions people ask most about how this site works, answered directly. Each answer below is the same answer published in this page's FAQ.

Do I pay more when I buy through your affiliate links?

No. You pay exactly the vendor's price — the commission comes out of the vendor's margin, not your pocket. Worth It Stack's links never add fees or change what you owe at checkout; if anything, they help keep every comparison on this site free to read.

Do higher commissions mean better rankings?

No. Commissions are set by each vendor's affiliate program, not negotiated per review — and vendors cannot pay for a better ranking, a softer criticism, or a spot in our comparisons. If the tool that pays us less is the better fit for you, that's the one we recommend.

How can I tell which links are affiliate links?

We disclose affiliate relationships in three places: on this dedicated disclosure page, in a visible disclosure box on every article page that contains affiliate links, and near the specific links themselves where practical. If anything about the business model is unclear, email hello@worthitstack.com and we'll answer plainly.

What this means in practice

Many of the product links on Worth It Stack are affiliate links. That means if you click through and sign up or buy, the vendor pays us a commission (typically a percentage of the sale, sometimes a flat fee or a recurring share for subscription products). You pay exactly the same price you'd pay going to the vendor directly — the commission comes out of the vendor's margin, not your pocket.

What it doesn't mean

  • It doesn't decide our verdicts. Commissions are set by each vendor's affiliate program, not negotiated per review. If the tool that pays us less is the better fit for you, that's the one we recommend. Our methodology documents how verdicts are actually reached.
  • It doesn't buy placement. Vendors cannot pay for a better ranking, a softer criticism, or a spot in our comparisons.
  • It doesn't cost you anything. Affiliate links never change the price you pay.

Where you'll see disclosure

In line with FTC guidelines on endorsements and testimonials, we disclose affiliate relationships:

  • On this dedicated page (you're reading it),
  • In a visible disclosure box on every article page that contains affiliate links,
  • Near the specific links themselves where practical.

Programs we participate in

Worth It Stack participates in affiliate programs with a number of the software vendors we cover — including, As of October 2026, Systeme.io and GoHighLevel. Where an affiliate relationship exists, links on this site may earn us a commission. Participation status never affects whether a tool is covered or how it's rated.
