The 7 alternatives, reviewed

1. systeme.io — the honest downgrade for solos

Price: Free plan (2,000 contacts) · Startup $17/mo · Webinar $47/mo · Unlimited $97/mo.

The alternative for people who bought an agency platform for a non-agency problem. systeme.io covers funnels, email, blogs, basic automation, and course hosting at $0–$17/month — a genuine 70–90% saving over GoHighLevel for a solo business with one offer and one mailing list. What it doesn't do: sub-accounts, white-labeling, CRM pipelines, two-way SMS, reputation management. If any of those are load-bearing for you, this isn't your alternative — it's the opposite of your problem.

The catch: you'll outgrow it the day you take on your second client. Budget for the eventual upgrade back up.

2. ActiveCampaign — the automation pick

Price: Starter from ~$29/mo (1,000 contacts) · scales to $419/mo at 10,000 contacts (Professional).

If the reason you bought GoHighLevel was marketing automation and the funnels/CRM white-labeling never mattered, ActiveCampaign's automation builder is arguably the best in the mid-market: nested conditions, split actions, goal-based triggers, and a genuinely good built-in CRM. The pricing model is the trade-off — it scales with contacts, so a growing list gets expensive fast. A 10,000-contact business pays $239–$419/month, at which point GoHighLevel's flat pricing looks cheap again.

The catch: contact-count pricing punishes growth; no funnel builder; no client sub-accounts.

3. Keap — the small-business classic

Price: Lite $79/mo · Pro $149–$169/mo · Max $199/mo.

Keap (formerly Infusionsoft) is the veteran of this list — battle-tested CRM + marketing automation for small businesses that want proven sequences, not shiny objects. The Lite tier undercuts GoHighLevel's entry while including a real CRM and automation library. But the platform shows its age next to modern builders, and it has none of GoHighLevel's agency resale infrastructure. Pick it when reliability beats novelty.

The catch: dated UX in places; $79 entry buys less funnel power than GoHighLevel's $97.

4. Kartra — the behavioral-automation pick

Price: Essentials $59/mo (500 contacts, 5% transaction fee) · runs to $549/mo (Professional) · 30-day free trial.

Kartra's behavioral automation — if/then sequences driven by page visits, video watch time, email engagement, and purchase behavior — remains the deepest on this list. The feature set (funnels, email, memberships, helpdesk, affiliate management, calendars) is genuinely comprehensive, and at $59/month Essentials it's a real saving for a solo business. The honest trade-off, consistent across 2026 reviews: the interface feels dated, and the learning curve is the steepest here after GoHighLevel itself. Pick it when automation sophistication is the job, not when you want pretty.

The catch: the 5% transaction fee on Essentials eats margins on high-ticket offers; dated UI.

5. Kajabi — the high-ticket pick

Price: Basic $179/mo ($143 annual) · Growth $249/mo · Pro $499/mo · 0% platform transaction fees on every plan.

The most polished course and coaching platform in the category, and the only one here where the brand itself signals premium to high-ticket buyers. Kajabi raised prices in January 2026 and eliminated its $89 entry plan, so the cheapest way in is $179/month — and the contact squeeze is real (Basic dropped to 2,500 contacts; affiliate programs, advanced automations, and API access wait until the $249/month Growth plan). Worth it for established coaches selling $2,000+ programs; absurd for a first course. If courses are the business and client management is secondary, this beats GoHighLevel.

The catch: expensive entry, contact limits tighten fast, no CRM pipeline depth.

6. HubSpot — the scale-up pick (read the fine print)

Price: Free CRM · Marketing Hub Starter ~$15–$20/mo · Marketing Hub Professional ~$890/mo + mandatory onboarding fee.

HubSpot appears on every "GoHighLevel alternative" list, and it's the most misleading entry on this list. The free CRM is genuinely excellent and the Starter tier is cheap — but real marketing automation is locked behind Professional at ~$890/month with a mandatory onboarding fee (recently ~$3,750). That's not a cheaper alternative; it's the expensive upgrade path. Switch to HubSpot when you're scaling up to a team with sales/marketing/service under one roof — never when you're trying to cut your bill.

The catch: the Starter tier lacks the automation people actually switch for; costs explode past it.

7. GoHighLevel itself — don't switch without reading this

Price: Starter $97/mo · Unlimited $297/mo · Agency Pro $497/mo, plus $20–$150/mo in usage fees.

The honest entry: nothing on this list does what GoHighLevel does for agencies. Sub-accounts, white-labeling, client rebilling, and SaaS mode (resell the platform under your own brand at your own pricing) are genuinely unique — no competitor offers the same resale infrastructure at a flat $497/month. Most "alternatives" are cheaper because they sell you less: fewer clients, simpler automation, no resale. If you're switching to save money while keeping the same agency workflow, you're shopping for something that doesn't exist.

What is the best free alternative to GoHighLevel?

Is there an alternative to GoHighLevel with SaaS mode?

No. GoHighLevel's SaaS mode — reselling the platform under your own brand with your own pricing — is genuinely unique. No competitor offers the same white-labeled resale infrastructure at a flat $497/month. If SaaS mode is the reason you bought GoHighLevel, there is no direct replacement.

Should I switch from GoHighLevel to HubSpot?

Only if you're scaling up, not down. HubSpot's free CRM and cheap Starter tier (~$15–20/month) look tempting, but real marketing automation sits behind Marketing Hub Professional at ~$890/month plus a mandatory onboarding fee. For most agencies, that's the opposite of a cheaper alternative.

Why are GoHighLevel's costs higher than the advertised price?

What is the best GoHighLevel alternative for solo creators?

systeme.io — free to start, or $17–$97/month paid, built for solo creators with one offer and simple funnels. GoHighLevel's value engine is sub-accounts, white-labeling, and client rebilling — infrastructure a solo creator with one funnel pays for but never touches.

Which GoHighLevel alternative is best if I only need automation?

ActiveCampaign — priced $29–$419 per month by contacts — is the pick when automation sophistication is the job and you don't need funnel building. Keap ($79–$199 per month) is the alternative for small businesses that live inside proven automations rather than building funnels themselves.

How long does it take to learn GoHighLevel?

Plan on two to four weeks before it clicks — that's the consistent finding in reviews. The setup cost only pays off if you actually use the platform's depth, so solo creators should weigh the learning curve against simpler tools like systeme.io before committing to the $97/month starting price.
